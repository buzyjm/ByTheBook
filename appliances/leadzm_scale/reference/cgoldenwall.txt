Ⅰ. Common Questions
(1) Q: What to do if the scale get inaccurate after some time of use?
A: Please calibrate it according to the instruction. (see in (4))

(2) Q: Why the reading is always fluctuating and it can’t set zero or get a
sable weighing results?
A: Please check whether your under-plate is installed reversed. The
incorrect installation will cause fluctuating reading.
See in part 1, how to install the under-plate correctly.

(3) Q: Why the 5000g scale is without calibration weights?
A: This lab scale with range from 200g-3000g all has calibration weights
except 5000g. Because the 5000g scale needs 2000g calibration weights,
which is too heavy and might damage the scale during international
shipment. If you need calibration weights, you can purchase on Amazon
easily.
Here is the link of 2kg calibration weights:
https://www.amazon.com/2000g-Calibration-Weight-Chrome-
Scales/dp/B 07SY8FV32/

2.Put the 500g calibration weights when the balance displays twinkles
500g.Remove the calibration after stabilization.If the scale displays
"0.00", it is finished.
(Friendly remind:Please kindly make sure that you place the balance on a
smooth and steady table and adjust the four black screws at the button to
make it stable.)

Ⅲ. Operation
1. Prepare for operation
Place the balance on a stable, level table and adjust the leveling feet to
make the level bubble in the circle(not necessarily in the center). Avoid
vibrations, sunlight,strong airflow, and strong electromagnetic
interference.

Connect an external power supply or install a 9V battery.
Press "ON/OFF" button to display 8.8.8.8.8."," maximum weighing value,
"---", "where" --- "The display time depends on the stability of the sensor.
Therefore, do not place the balance on the unstable worktable. Finally, the
weighing mode “0 or 0.0 or 000” is displayed. If the “O” symbol in the
upper left corner shines, the workplace is stable.

2. Calibration Balance
(1)Place the balance on a stable, level table and avoid vibration, sunlight,
airflow, and strong electromagnetic interference.

(2)Calibration process
Hold down the "CAL calibration" button without holding any object on
the weighing pan. After about 3 seconds, immediately release the

7. Overweight Alarm
   When the mass of the object is said to exceed (1+2%) of the value
displayed on the display, the horizontal "---" sign indicates that the
accumulative item exceeds the specified range, and the scale should be
removed immediately, otherwise it will damage the balance.

(2) The tare weight and the weight of the object must not exceed the

(3) If the weighing is not accurate, use standard weights to calibrate the
    balance.

(4) To remove the weighing pan from the balance, turn the weighing pan
    clockwise and remove it. Do not push the weighing pan upwards to
    avoid damaging the sensor.
